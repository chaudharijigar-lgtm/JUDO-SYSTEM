🏆 Tournament details
Shown on every printed sheet, on the QR pages and on the TV scoreboard header. Saved with backups.
⏱ Contest time by age group
Set automatically on the scoreboard whenever a match is loaded from the draw (the scoreboard's own "Match time" is only for manual matches). Golden score has no time limit.
⏳ Golden score limit
IJF rules: golden score has no time limit (default) and only a score (yuko, waza-ari, ippon) or hansoku-make ends it; shido never decides. For young age groups a limit can be set: when it runs out the clock stops and the referees decide (Hantei) with the ⚖ Decision button on the scoreboard.

🖨 What to print

A4 landscape · one page per category (big draws split into pools) · print stage 1 before the event, stage 2 once elimination is complete.
1. Athletes page → Download template and fill it in Excel (or use your own sheet with the same column names).
2. Click Upload Excel / CSV.
3. Pick a weight category on the left and click Make draw.
4. Click the winner's name in each match. Winners move forward to the final automatically.
